Race Energy & Highlights
* Turn 1 Carnage: Fernando Alonso spun after late-braking the damp Turn 1, triggering a multi-car pileup that ended Yuki Tsunoda and Daniel Ricciardo's races before Lap 2.

* Weather Chaos: Heavy showers hit midway, prompting a mad tire scramble—George Russell bet on inters one lap sooner than rivals, vaulting from P9 to P4.

* Wildlife Invasion: The official broadcast caught a kangaroo hopping along the T10 safety fencing during the Lap 32 safety car, leading to instant social trending.

* Strategy Mayhem: Oscar Piastri’s McLaren boxed twice in five laps—chasing the double undercut that put him in podium contention despite dodging debris.

* Surprise Podium: Alex Albon took P3 after Max Verstappen retired late with power failure, sending Williams social admins into overdrive.

* Notable Absences: Carlos Sainz missed the event due to appendicitis, replaced by F2’s Ollie Bearman—Bearman’s P11 finish turned into the paddock’s meme story.
